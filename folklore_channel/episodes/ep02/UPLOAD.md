[S1] Basil Johnston, "The Manitous: The Spiritual World of the Ojibway" (HarperCollins, 1995), chapter "Weendigo": a giant, gaunt, ash-grey figure with torn lips; it grows as it eats, so it is never full; a teaching against excess and greed.
