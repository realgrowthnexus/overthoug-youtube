## Cadence decision: 4 long videos in month 1, one every Friday
- **Not 2:** a brand-new channel has no subscribers, so each upload is one lottery ticket in Browse and Suggested. With only 2 videos, YouTube gets too few chances to find our audience, and we learn nothing from the A/B thumbnails.
- **Not 8:** quality is what makes this format go viral. Dinzo posts rarely and still gets millions of views because each video is strong. More than one a week would mean weaker scripts.
- **Weekly on a fixed day:** it builds a habit ("new one every Friday", which is in the banner). By week 4, a viewer who finds any video has 3 more to binge, and long-session binges are what push a small channel into Suggested.
- **Length: 9–11 minutes.** That's long enough for mid-roll ads later (8+ min) and long enough for strong watch time, but still short enough to keep retention above about 45%. Each script is about 1,400–1,500 words.
- **Shorts:** one 45-second vertical cut of each hook, posted 2 days after the long video, with the long video linked as the "related video". Shorts bring in new viewers. They don't replace the long videos.

## What makes them click, and what makes them stay
**Thumbnail rules** (all 8 follow them):
1. One gritty character, big, on the left half.
2. 2–3 handwritten words that open a question, with the last word in red.
3. An arrow to the subject.
4. White everywhere else, so it pops in a dark feed.
5. Never repeat the title words in the thumbnail.

**Hook rules** (the first 30 seconds of every script):
1. Line 1 drops you into the worst moment ("You are seven years old…").
2. Stakes or a contradiction by second 15 ("a law already says you shouldn't be here").
3. A promise of a twist later ("by the end you'll find out…").
4. Only then, "Let's go back to how you got here."

**Retention beats:**
- A pattern break every 60–90 seconds (a twist, "here's the part that will make you angry").
- A callback in the outro.
- The endcard teases the next episode by name.

- **Every Monday:**
  - Check click-through rate (aim for 6% or more) and the 30-second retention (aim for 70% or more).
  - If click-through is under 4% after 48 h, swap the thumbnail even if Test & Compare is still running.
- **End of month:** look at which topic did best and double down. The pinned comment on EP04 asks viewers to vote for the next life.
