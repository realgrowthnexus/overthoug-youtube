## 7. One-time steps (do these today)
1. **Verify your phone number** (Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → Intermediate features). Without it, you can't upload custom thumbnails or videos longer than 15 minutes.
2. **Create the playlist** "Why It Sucks to Be Born as… (Human History)".
3. **Turn on 2-step verification** on the Google account, so the channel is harder to hijack.
4. **Monetization:** the YouTube Partner Program needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours, or 10M Shorts views. Apply as soon as you hit it.
